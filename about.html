About ShelfCycle

ShelfCycle is a community bookshelf. We believe good books deserve another chapter — instead of gathering dust, they should move from reader to reader. Browse the catalog, rate what you've read, suggest what's missing, and pass your own books along through swaps, lends, and school donations.

What you can do here

Browse the catalog Search by title, author, or ISBN and filter by language, length, rating, and publication year.
Rate & suggest Give books a star rating, and suggest anything missing — scan the barcode and we'll fill in the details for you.
Swap & lend List a book you own to swap or lend, and request books from other members.
Donate to schools Send a book to a classroom and see it join the public donations list.
Track your reading Your profile keeps your want-to-read, currently reading, finished, and did-not-finish shelves — plus a yearly reading challenge.
Find local libraries The map shows every public library and Little Free Library inside New York's 18th congressional district.

How it works

New book suggestions go to a short review queue before appearing in the catalog, which keeps the shelves tidy. Accounts are free — sign up on the home page to save lists, track progress, and join the swap shelf.

Book details come from the Open Library and library locations from OpenStreetMap, two wonderful open-data projects.
